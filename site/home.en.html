A calm coach for training, sleep and food

Training, sleep and food are connected, so Novila looks at them together and tells you what to do next today, and why. Everything stays on your phone.

Coming soon to Google Play

Everything stays on your phone

Novila doesn't even have permission to use the internet. No account, no ads and no analytics. You can save your own copy and delete everything whenever you like.

Every suggestion has a reason

Novila has no AI. The plan and the insights come from clear rules that explain themselves: why today's session is lighter, and how many nights a link is based on. A link is a link, never proof.

No pressure

Habits count in whole weeks, not days, so a missed day breaks nothing. More training is never worth more, and rest days count too. No red numbers and no streaks to lose.

Training

  • A weekly program following the WHO's advice on cardio and strength, built around your days, your time and the equipment you have, or just your body weight
  • A workout mode with sets, rest and countdowns, on your Wear OS watch too
  • A drawn figure shows every exercise and the muscles at work
  • Gentle training in three levels, focused on knee, lower back, shoulder, neck or hip

Sleep

  • Log your night yourself, or read it from your watch through Health Connect
  • See how regularly you sleep and what seems to go with your nights: late coffee, training, food and water
  • Short breathing exercises for the evening

Food

  • A food score built on the Swedish Food Agency's dietary advice, without counting calories
  • Water and other drinks, glass by glass
  • More than 40 recipes for your diet and allergens, and room for your own

Steps, workouts, nights, resting heart rate and HRV can be read from Health Connect, only while the app is open and only what you have allowed. Novila writes nothing there and saves nothing from it. You can also log everything yourself, so you don't need a watch.

Novila is made for adults and gives general advice on training, sleep and food. It is not a medical device. Contact a healthcare provider about pain, injury or problems that don't go away.
